# 🦄 Unicorn Stampede

**Six unicorns. Three towns. One very bad day for local architecture.**

Control one unicorn at a time while managing the whole herd. Paint the town with rainbow trails, collect power-ups, rescue captured unicorns, team up to smash stronger buildings, and destroy the Town Hall before the town fights back.

## Controls

- **WASD / Arrows** — Move
- **Shift** — Switch unicorn
- **Click near your unicorn** — Crack the whip and build charge
- **Space** — Dash / smash
- **P / Esc** — Pause
- **M** — Mute

Clicking near the currently controlled unicorn triggers the **whip mechanic**. Successful whip timing builds charge, which strengthens your next dash. Build up charge, then press **Space** to launch a stronger smash through the town.

Your other unicorns keep moving when you're not controlling them. They can wander, chase opportunities, get distracted, or end up in danger, so switching at the right moment matters.

The town pushes back with cleaners, traffic, capture vehicles, rebuilding, and stronger defenses. If **3 unicorns are captured, it's game over**.

## Three worlds

**Prismborough** — classic town streets and civic landmarks.  
**Washwater Bay** — sandy coastal routes, marina pressure, and harbor chaos.  
**Cloudtop Heights** — tighter stone paths and crosswinds that shove the herd around.

Power-ups can turn the tide, but stronger objectives often require multiple unicorns nearby. Sometimes the best move is smashing a building. Sometimes it's abandoning the fight to rescue a straggler across town.
